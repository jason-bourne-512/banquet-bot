  • "Biggest meal" = add up every breakfast across all rooms, then every lunch; the bigger of the two sets the crew. Breakfast and lunch staff the same way.
  • Small days run richer (1 per 16–20), because someone has to cover the room no matter what. Big days get leaner, around 1 per 30. Your 1-per-35 guess is close for the big days.
  • Receptions run very lean: 1 server up to ~150 guests, about 3 for 200, 3–5 for 300.
  • Dinners: only 3 in the data. A 30-person plated dinner got 2.
  • Number of rooms barely matters. Meals spread over 3+ rooms ran about 1:21 vs 1:23 in one room.
  • Day of the week doesn't matter; only the events do.

Shift times

  • Morning crew starts 2 hours before the first meal (7:30 breakfast → 5:30A). This held on 191 shifts.
  • Morning crew leaves ~1½ hours after the last lunch ends (lunch done 1:00 → out ~2:30P).
  • Evening crew starts ~2 hours before a reception or dinner (5:30 reception → 3:30P) and stays about an hour after it ends.
