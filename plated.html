Stand-up, prep, set, serve and clear — tap a step to open
Before doors
🗣
Stand-up (pre-shift)
▶
What to cover
  • Post as — what the event is (the function on the EO).
  • Organization — who it’s for, and anything else we know about the group.
  • Contact — who the on-site contact is.
  • Timeline — what time we should be done.
  • Number of guests.
  • Go over the menu and every dietary restriction.
  • Teams, roles and expectations.
  • Sections — put the best servers in the contact’s section.
  • Table settings — walk through the diagram and what each table should look like.
  • End-of-night tasks.
Say it like this

“Stay focused from the time you clock in to the time we’re done. Leave the long conversations for afterwards. Some people assume management are the brains and you are the labor — but we need everyone’s brains. Everyone actively engaged and aware.”

  • “We start strong on setup and lose focus on clean-up. Stay focused, work like a team. Don’t cut corners or ignore dirty dishes.”
  • “Don’t think ‘that’s not my responsibility’ or ‘that’s not my section.’ We need everyone on board, everyone’s eyes, helping each other.”
  • “If your tables are cleared, I might grab a couple of you for side tasks to help us clean up faster.”
  • “We eat once the talk starts — half eat while the other half cleans the back of house, then we switch.”
  • “Sections ready 30 minutes before doors open.”
  • “At your sections, ready to go, 15 minutes before doors open.”
  • “Any questions or comments, come to me. Thank you.”
🧺
Prep
▶
  • Napkins — waterfold.
  • Wipe glassware — water, wine, tea and bar glasses (microfiber cloth).
  • Salt & pepper sets.
  • Wipe utensils — dinner knives, salad and dinner forks, dessert and tea spoons.
  • Bread baskets with white linen.
  • B&B (butter) plates and butter knives — set in the banquet walk-in.
  • Goosenecks for dressing, on B&Bs with serving spoons.
  • Coffee trays — cups, saucers, (2) sugar caddies, teaspoons.
  • Brew coffee; creamers.
  • Brew tea, and have a backup.
  • Fill water glasses — ice first, then water.
  • Water and tea pitchers.
  • Tray jacks out of the room → back in once guests are seated.
Set
🍽
Setting the table
▶
  • Set silverware to the diagram — your table should match your neighbor’s and face the way the map shows.
  • Sugar caddies.
  • Salt and pepper — salt on the right.
  • Centerpiece, mirrors, candles — wipe them all.
  • B&B plate, coffee cup and saucer.
  • Wine, water and tea glasses.

Teamwork check: walk your row. Every table should look identical.

Service
🍷
Serve
▶
  • Folded napkin on your arm, standing at your station 15 minutes before start.
  • Know the dietary restrictions on the menu (GF, CD).
  • Ask guests about dietary restrictions — tell the captain right away.
  • Serve the entrée only once all salads are cleared.
  • Keep refilling water, tea and wine.
  • Serve left, clear right.
  • Carry trays.
🧹
Clearing
▶
  • Clear from the right.
  • Utensils and plates first.
  • On the tray: plates outside, utensils inside; scrape onto one plate.
  • Then napkins, then glassware — water, tea, then coffee.
  • Have a scraper with you.
🚿
Back of house
▶
  • Set up a breakdown station: trash, splash bucket, bus tubs, linen buckets, Queen Mary.
  • Racks for water, wine, rocks and coffee glasses.
  • Don’t let the trash get too full — tie it off and start fresh.
  • Don’t let the splash bucket get too full.
  • Take plates on the Queen Mary to dish once the ballroom is 90% clear.
