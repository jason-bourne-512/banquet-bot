How signs are built
Every left room goes on one LEFT-arrow sign (Lantana, Plumeria, Primrose A and B). Every right room goes on one RIGHT-arrow sign (Primrose C and D, Camellia, Verbena). Bluebell and Buttercup each get their own directional, right arrow unless you change it. Door signs: a full room gets 2, a single section gets 1, Primrose A gets 1, Primrose B gets 2, Primrose C and D get 1 each, Bluebell and Buttercup get none. Room name, arrow, label and count are all editable. Directional only? Use × door sign on a room, or untick Door signs for the whole group. Door sign only? Use × arrow on a room, or untick Directional. Two directionals with the same arrow can print as one page: use Combine directionals. A group with more than one room: use + Add room on its card (pick a room or type your own), and each room becomes another line under the name on the directional. The × next to a room takes it off the signs. ⋯ on a room has its door label and the × arrow / × door sign switches.
