PRINT SIGNS

Directionals and door signs built from the Rundown for the date you pick. Check the names and counts, then print.

Print Signs is for Jason and Hector only.
Rules: left of the map is a LEFT arrow (Lantana, Plumeria, Primrose A and B). Right is a RIGHT arrow (Primrose C and D, Camellia, Verbena). Bluebell gets its own directional with a right arrow. Door signs: a full room gets 2, a single section gets 1, Primrose A gets 2, Primrose C and D get 1 each. Counts marked guess are not confirmed and can be edited. Set a count to 0 to skip a door sign.
