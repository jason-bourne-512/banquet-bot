Quick reference
⚡
At a glance
▶
  • Most ordered: Vodka Soda · Jack & Coke · Margarita · Gin & Tonic · Rum & Coke
  • Pour: 1.5 oz standard · 2 oz for neat or spirit-forward cocktails
  • Gluten-free: Austin Eastciders
  • Guest asks for bourbon: Jack Daniel’s (technically Tennessee whiskey)
  • Not on the list: “We’re running a hosted bar tonight — can I make you something with one of these?”

The hotel pours Pepsi products — “& Coke” drinks are made with Pepsi.

Cocktails
▶
Bourbon
  • Jack & Coke — Jack Daniel’s, cola, lime. Rocks.
  • Old Fashioned — Jack Daniel’s, simple syrup, Angostura bitters, orange peel. Large cube.
  • Whiskey Sour — Jack Daniel’s, lemon juice, simple syrup. Shaken, rocks.
Vodka
  • Vodka Soda — Tito’s, soda, lime. Highball.
  • Vodka Tonic — Tito’s, tonic, lime. Highball.
  • Vodka Cranberry — Tito’s, cranberry, lime. Rocks.
  • Cosmopolitan — Tito’s, triple sec, lime juice, cranberry. Shaken, martini glass.
Tequila
  • Margarita — Cuervo Gold, lime juice, triple sec. Shaken, rocks. Salt rim optional.
  • Tequila Soda — Cuervo Gold, soda, lime. Highball.
Gin
  • Gin & Tonic — Tanqueray, tonic, lime. Highball.
  • Gin Soda — Tanqueray, soda, lime. Lighter, drier.
Rum
  • Rum & Coke — Bacardi, cola, lime. Rocks.
  • Daiquiri — Bacardi, lime juice, simple syrup. Shaken, coupe.
  • Mojito — Bacardi, lime juice, simple syrup, fresh mint, soda. Highball.
Scotch
  • Neat — Dewar’s, 2 oz, rocks glass, no ice.
  • On the rocks — Dewar’s, one large cube.
  • Scotch & Soda — Dewar’s, soda. Highball.
🍷
Wine
▶

  • Vista Point Cabernet Sauvignon — Full red — dark fruit, soft tannins. Room temperature. Red wine glass.
