Company Overview

About White Lodging
▶

White Lodging is one of the fastest-growing independent hotel ownership, development and management groups in the US — operating brands including Marriott, Hyatt, Hilton, IHG, Preferred, and Choice. Founded 1985 in Merrillville, Indiana. Now 165+ properties in 19 states.

Vision

To become the most desired and highly valued developer, owner and operator of leading brand full and select service hotels.

Your Rights & Policies
⚖️
Equal Employment & Anti-Harassment
▶

White Lodging is an Equal Employment Opportunity employer. Discrimination or harassment based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex, age, disability, or any protected category is strictly prohibited.

Zero Tolerance

Harassment includes offensive remarks, jokes, images, sexual advances, or physical conduct. Violation = immediate termination.

How to Report
  • Talk to your HR Department or General Manager
  • Contact Corporate Associate Relations: (219) 472-2900
  • Anonymous: (888) 307-2798 or anonymous.alertline.com

No retaliation for reporting. You will not be penalized for making a good-faith complaint.

🚪
Open Door Policy
▶

Any concern can be raised through the Problem-Solving Procedure:

  • Step 1: Discuss with your direct Manager
  • Step 2: Escalate to your Department Leader
  • Step 3: HR Department or General Manager
  • Step 4: Corporate Associate Relations (219) 472-2900

Anonymous option always available: (888) 307-2798 · supports multiple languages

🚫
Drug & Alcohol Policy
▶

Zero tolerance. Reporting to work under the influence or possessing illegal substances on property = immediate termination.

Testing Occasions
  • Pre-employment (all new hires)
  • Reasonable suspicion
  • Post-accident (where applicable)
  • Random (where permitted by law)
Substances Tested

Cocaine, PCP, Amphetamines, Opiates. Marijuana tested if driving company vehicle or reasonable suspicion exists.

Associates with substance abuse issues are encouraged to seek help before any misconduct occurs. Resources: National Drug Abuse Hotline (800) 662-HELP.

🔒
Workplace Security
▶

Violence or threats of violence on White Lodging premises = immediate termination.

Firearms

Prohibited on all White Lodging property except: licensed concealed carry in personal vehicle, kept out of sight in locked compartment. Law enforcement must notify GM in writing.

Guest Privacy Rules
  • Never give out guest names or room numbers
  • Never issue keys without proper ID
  • Never mention room numbers aloud
  • Robbery: surrender all money, call 911
Pay & Benefits
💵
Payroll & Pay Periods
▶
  • Paid bi-weekly — every other Friday
  • Workweek: Saturday–Friday
  • Pay period closes 7 days before payday
  • Direct deposit to personal bank account or pay card
  • No pay advances under any circumstances
Overtime

All hours over 40/week are overtime. Must get manager approval before working overtime. Only actual hours worked count — not paid time off.

Tip Reporting

Federal law requires reporting tips over $20/month. Failure to report may result in IRS penalties. Use Form 4070 to track daily tips.

🏥
Benefits Overview
▶
Full-Time (130+ hrs/month)
  • Medical, Dental, Vision: 1st of month after 60 days (non-mgmt) or after hire (mgmt)
  • Life Insurance: after 90 days
  • 401(k): after 1 year + 1,000 hours worked

May qualify for full-time benefits by averaging 130 hrs/month in first 11 months OR working 3 consecutive months at 130+ hrs.

🌿
Leaves of Absence
▶
FMLA Eligibility
  • 12+ months with White Lodging
  • 1,250+ hours in the past 12 months
  • Work location has 50+ associates within 75 miles
FMLA Reasons
  • Birth or adoption of a child (up to 12 weeks)
  • Serious health condition — yours or immediate family
  • Military caregiver leave (up to 26 weeks)
  • Qualifying military exigency
Personal Leave

Up to 30 days unpaid for full-time associates after 1 year. Must request 2 weeks in advance in writing. Cannot hold other employment while on leave.

Military Leave

Unpaid time off to fulfill military duties. Provide orders as soon as possible.

Work Rules & Conduct
⏰
Attendance & Scheduling
▶
  • Be in full uniform at your workstation at your scheduled start time
  • Verify your schedule weekly — shifts can change based on business needs
  • Schedule changes require manager approval — no unauthorized shift swaps
  • Schedules posted no later than 2 days before workweek starts

3 consecutive no-call/no-shows = immediate termination.

Time Clock Rules
  • Clock in no more than 5 minutes before scheduled shift
  • Clock out no more than 5 minutes after shift ends
  • Never clock in/out for another associate
  • Report punching errors to manager immediately
📋
Progressive Discipline
▶
  • Verbal Warning — documented, placed in personnel file
  • Written Warning — associate signs (signing confirms receipt, not agreement)
  • Suspension or Termination — for serious violations or 3 written warnings in 12 months

Some violations result in immediate termination without prior warnings: theft, fraud, violence, harassment, drug/alcohol violations.

All warnings remain in personnel file for duration of employment.

📌
Basic Work Rules
▶

Violations of any of these may result in discipline up to and including immediate termination:

  • Theft — any unauthorized removal of property
  • Fraud / False statements — falsifying documents, timecards, or applications
  • Insubordination — refusing reasonable manager directions
  • Sleeping on the job
  • Gambling on property or company time
  • Damage to property — deliberate or reckless
  • Confidentiality breach — sharing guest, associate, or company information
  • Leaving property without permission during work hours
  • Unsafe work practices — horseplay, ignoring safety rules
  • Unauthorized recording — photos, video, audio on property

Lost & Found: Turn in all found items to manager same day. Keeping guest items = termination.

📈
Promotions & Transfers
▶

White Lodging strongly supports promotions from within. To request a job change:

  • Must have been in current role for at least 6 months
  • No written warnings in the last 6 months
  • Complete "Request for Promotion/Transfer" form signed by manager
Dress Code & Appearance
👔
Uniform & Grooming Standards
▶
Uniform
  • Clean and in good repair at all times
  • Slip-resistant shoes, clean and polished
  • Nametag worn at all times during work
  • Undergarments worn and not visible
  • No jeans, shorts, tank tops, sandals unless job-specific
  • Visible tattoos must not be vulgar or offensive
Jewelry
  • Max 2 rings per hand
  • No necklaces outside uniform
  • One watch per arm
  • Max 2 earrings per ear, no extreme styles
Hair

Clean and neatly maintained. Must not cover eyes. If long, pull back. Food handling positions may require hairnet. Males: mustaches and beards acceptable.

Food Handling

No nail polish or artificial nails unless wearing intact gloves. Wash hands after restroom, eating, smoking, touching face/hair.

Tobacco & Smoking

Only in designated outdoor areas. Includes e-cigarettes and vaping.

Phones, Privacy & Separation
📱
Cell Phones & Personal Calls
▶
  • Personal calls: before/after shift or on break only
  • Non-White Lodging devices not permitted in areas where credit card or confidential information is stored/processed
  • While driving on White Lodging business: no talking or texting — comply with all local laws
🚶
Separation of Employment
▶
  • Please give 2 weeks written notice when resigning
  • Final paycheck issued on next regular payday after separation
  • Request any earned vacation/PTO before your last day
  • Return all White Lodging property on your last day
  • Rehire = start as a new associate
